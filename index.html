How It Works
Tap this icon (top-left of the map) to enter Background mode.
Start with 📐 Plot — draw a rectangle over your whole garden, then enter its real width and length in meters. This sets the scale that every bed and shape's size is measured against, so it's worth doing first.
Then add Rectangle, Circle, or Tree shapes for lawn, beds, trees, and paths. Choose a plain color or a Grass/Earth/Rocks texture. Drag a shape's middle to move it, a corner to resize. Tap "Done" when you're happy with the layout.
Back on the main map, tap "+ Add Bed" to drop a new bed at the center of your view. Drag its middle to move it — it snaps to nearby beds and shapes — or drag a corner to resize.
Tap this icon (top-right of a card) to record what's planted, key dates, what's coming next, and any notes.
Inside a bed's popup, tap its color to pick from 5 simple presets, and tap the small pencil next to its name to rename it.
The Priorities tab lists everything that needs seeding, planting, or harvesting soon.
The Plant Guide has details for 100+ vegetables, flowers, and herbs — including spacing, companions, and a calendar view.
